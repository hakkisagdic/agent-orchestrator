**Operational debt held against #40 — and a correction.** On 2026-09-07 the Voltrai doctor job
was unloaded (`launchctl bootout gui/501/com.agentorchestrator.doctor.voltrai`) because every
finding it produced went to the human channels every fifteen minutes, and three false red e-mails
arrived in one minute. **That unload did not hold.** Measured 2026-09-14: `launchctl list` shows
`com.agentorchestrator.doctor.voltrai` loaded again, last exit 1. A plist left in
`~/Library/LaunchAgents/` is re-bootstrapped by launchd on the next login, so `bootout` alone is a
pause, not a stop — and a human who believes a job is off while it is on will misread every alert
it sends. That is why the notifications kept arriving after the fix: the job never stopped running
`ao doctor --check` on a fifteen-minute timer, and `--check` pages a person by design.

The real correction is #40 (a command a person or an agent runs never pages anyone), and the
recorded operational fact is now the opposite of what this paragraph used to say: nothing needs
re-loading. If it must genuinely be stopped before #40, the plist has to be disabled
(`launchctl disable gui/501/com.agentorchestrator.doctor.voltrai`) or moved out of
`~/Library/LaunchAgents/`; the undo is:

```
launchctl enable gui/501/com.agentorchestrator.doctor.voltrai
launchctl bootstrap gui/501 ~/Library/LaunchAgents/com.agentorchestrator.doctor.voltrai.plist
```

Landing #40 without confirming which of the two states the job is actually in leaves the fix
